# ADR 002: Temporal for durable execution

Status: accepted for the bootstrap.

## Decision

Use one self-hosted Temporal/Python implementation, app-owned evidence and scoped on-demand runners.

## Consequences and rejected alternatives

DBOS is superseded. Accept an additional service rather than build a distributed recovery engine. Activities remain idempotent/reconciled; no exactly-once side-effect promise.

## Reconsideration

Revisit only with concrete compatibility, measured resource/maintenance, licensing or
engineering evidence. Record the changed requirement and affected contracts. A coding
agent's familiarity with another stack is not sufficient reason to reopen the choice.

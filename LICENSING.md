# Licensing and distribution policy

## Core

Original application code and public documentation are licensed under AGPL-3.0-only
unless an applicable file declares another license. `LICENSE` contains the license text.
This policy does not grant rights in material owned by others.

## Independent rights

Third-party libraries, adapted predecessor code, fonts, CAD libraries, models, catalogs,
recipes, and data retain their own licenses and notices. Before an import, record the
exact source revision, content hashes, origin, license evidence, modifications, and
permitted distribution. Unknown rights block distribution of the affected content;
private possession or access to a repository is not a license grant.

Independently authored engineering records, credentials, private organizational catalogs,
and deployment configuration are not made public merely by this software license.
Generated outputs require their own input/asset rights review. Do not automatically
label all outputs AGPL, or assume outputs cannot embed separately licensed material.

## Extensions

Versioned APIs, manifests, and signed messages are preferred interfaces for independent
integrations. An interface or process boundary alone is not a legal exemption. Modular
packaging does not waive obligations applying to a combined or derived work.

No blanket proprietary in-process plugin exception is granted. Any additional permission
must be an explicit reviewed grant from the relevant rights holders. Independently
licensed packages retain their terms subject to applicable combination obligations.

## Shipping

Before distributing an image, source bundle, viewer, or release, retain required notices,
check dependencies and asset rights, and provide corresponding source as required by the
applicable licenses, including AGPL network-use obligations. The implemented release
must offer access to the exact applicable source, not an unrelated latest branch.

Adapted predecessor implementation and bundled library notices are recorded in `NOTICE`.
No predecessor catalogs, predecessor fonts, or third-party engineering models are included;
connector face outlines adapt only dimensions from one CC0-1.0 predecessor drawing (see `NOTICE`).
The web interface bundles the Geist and Geist Mono typefaces (SIL OFL 1.1) from the
Fontsource packages; their notice is recorded in `NOTICE` and served with the build.
The full AGPL text is reproduced verbatim; its own license-document notice is retained.

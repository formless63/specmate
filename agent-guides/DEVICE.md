# Assembly, make and review workflow

Compose exact board, enclosure, hardware and harness revisions. Track contextual occurrence
paths for repeated nested assemblies. Keep canonical placement separate from exploded/open
views and preserve the same selection across scene, tree, diagram and BOM.

Use declared geometry/requirements for mechanical proposals and wiring routes. Missing
models, tolerances, materials or process data constrain fit claims. Evaluate configuration
and compatibility by facts/rules and carry unknown or development-needed work explicitly.

Choose the correct contents/procurement/consumable BOM. Bought populated boards do not
also require purchasing their internal components. Stock reservations and consumption use
distinct workshop authority; optional prices never block ordinary preparation.

Record actual units/batches, consumption/returns, firmware assignments and measurements
with their procedures/configurations. One unit's test is not a blanket revision approval.
Prepare a reviewed release/export closure and expose unresolved items. Never turn a build
or review package into a purchase, flashing or printer operation implicitly.

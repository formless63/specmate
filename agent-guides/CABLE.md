# Stock and cable workflow

Discover permitted library, draft, configuration, checking and export operations. Begin
with stock construction and declared dimensions/ratings/sources. A standalone stock request
does not need endpoints or a complete device. Keep hierarchical conductor/layer identities.

For a cable, resolve endpoint bodies separately from pinouts/orientation/electrical contracts.
Propose reviewed matches, topology, lengths, shielding/bonds and an explicit preparation
profile. Missing allowances remain unknown. Workshop practices such as drain treatment
must not be assumed universal or inferred from a preview.

Inspect traces using the requested electrical semantics. Copper, resistance, capacitance,
switch conditions and active behavior are distinct. Resolve branch/joint and shield intent
before treating an illustrative pigtail as a connection.

Generate coordinated section, pin/color table, schematic, cut/preparation, BOM, labels,
continuity/isolation intent and preview from an exact snapshot. Inspect conflicts, missing
facts and scale/calibration for formboards. Freeze/release only with the relevant authority.
A cable-only package must stand alone at its interfaces. Report generation/check results
separately from actual cable manufacture and measured continuity.
